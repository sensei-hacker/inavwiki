# Introduction

This tab allows you to adjust the parameters of each of the physical outputs of your aircraft. In the Mixer Tab, you indicate how the sticks on your transmitter are mapped to the multiple servos and motors. The Outputs Tab lets you set parameters for each individual servo and the communication protocol for your motors.

* [Set ESC Motor Protocol and additional Servo Settings](https://github.com/iNavFlight/inav/blob/master/docs/ESC%20and%20servo%20outputs.md)
